In accordance with Section 96 and 131 of the Securities Act, 2015 and Clause 5.6.1(a) of the Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited Regulations we hereby convey the following information:

We are pleased to announce that Treet Battery Limited has launched its lithium-ion battery product line under the brand “Lithion NeoPower”, marking an important step in the Company’s continued focus on innovation, product diversification and advanced energy storage solutions.

The Lithion NeoPower product line includes modern battery energy storage solutions, comprising Residential Energy Storage System Series and Lead-Acid Replacement Series. The Residential Energy Storage Systems Series integrates rooftop solar, a hybrid inverter, battery storage and an intelligent energy management system. The Lead-Acid Replacement Series offers a modern lithium battery solution designed to seamlessly replace traditional lead-acid batteries, by utilizing advanced lithium cell technology and improved battery management systems these batteries deliver significantly higher energy efficiency, longer cycle life and improved reliability compared to conventional lead-acid solutions.

This launch complements the Company’s existing battery business and is expected to strengthen its product portfolio, enabling the Company to cater to the growing demand for reliable, efficient and future-ready energy storage solutions across residential and commercial sectors.
